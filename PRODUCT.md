# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Multi-persona internal BI tool serving one ERPNext-backed manufacturing company (CoaleTech / jkmchem deployment):

- **Business/ops managers** — finance, sales, procurement, HR leads reviewing dashboards at a desk during normal work hours, on desktop monitors.
- **Executives** — glancing at summary metrics on the move, low patience for detail, need the headline read in seconds.
- **Data analysts** — power users building queries and charts, long focused sessions, need density over glanceability.

The same screens serve all three: a 30-second glance and a 30-minute deep-analysis session, without a separate "simple mode."

## Product Purpose

AI-powered business intelligence platform built on Frappe/ERPNext. Turns ERP data (financial, sales, procurement, HR, manufacturing, customer) into intelligence dashboards, AI agents, and ML-driven analytics. Success looks like: a user trusts a number enough to act on it immediately, and can trace it back to source without asking anyone.

## Brand Personality

Precise, confident, technical. Reads like Linear or Raycast: dense information presented with total confidence, sharp typography, restrained color used deliberately, zero decorative fluff. Not a consumer app, not a marketing surface — a serious instrument.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS dashboard template.** The hero-metric-card grid: big number, small label, gradient accent, repeated identically across every panel. The default an AI-generated dashboard reaches for.
- **Consumer-app playful/rounded.** No bubbly illustrations, mascots, oversized rounded corners, or a "fun app" register. This is a serious internal tool people rely on to run a company.

## Design Principles

1. **Numbers earn trust through precision, not decoration.** Every value traceable to its source; no ambiguous formatting, no rounding that hides the real figure, no currency literals divorced from the ledger they came from.
2. **Density with clarity.** This serves both a 30-second glance and a 30-minute analysis session. Hierarchy has to work at both reading speeds on the same screen.
3. **Restraint as confidence.** Color and visual weight are earned signals — a risk state, an alert, a real deviation — not default styling applied everywhere.
4. **One tool, many personas.** No separate "exec view" vs "analyst view." The same dashboard serves both without redesign; density is managed through hierarchy, not simplification.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline. Standard contrast ratios, full keyboard navigation, screen-reader support. No additional constraints beyond standard practice for an internal business tool.
